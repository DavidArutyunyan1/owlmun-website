Committees

OWLMUN 2026 will feature five exciting committees:

  • General Assembly: A large-scale committee for global discussions on key issues.
  • Specialized Committees: Two specialized committees focusing on unique global challenges.
  • Crisis Committees: Fast-paced, reactive committees, one of which will be an ADHOC committee.
